DEEP VAULT

An original VR horror fangame. You are an ape that moves with its arms. Grab the loot in the abandoned vault, avoid the Hollow, and reach the exit. It hears your microphone and your movement, so stay quiet.

VR: hold grip or trigger and pull your hand back to drag yourself along. The left stick also moves you. The right hand holds the flashlight. Press trigger to retry after dying.
Desktop: W A S D to move, arrow keys to turn, R to restart.
